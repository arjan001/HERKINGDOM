# Her Kingdom - Jewelry & Accessories Store

## Brand Overview
Her Kingdom is a curated jewelry and accessory brand based in Nairobi, Kenya. We specialize in hypoallergenic, long-lasting jewelry pieces that complement personal style and embody individuality.

## Core Categories
- **Necklaces**: Curated chain necklaces, pendants, layered necklaces, chokers
- **Jewelry Sets**: Matching necklace and earring sets for complete looks
- **Bracelets**: Charm bracelets, bangles, cuffs, beaded bracelets
- **Earrings**: Stud earrings, hoops, drop earrings, statement pieces
- **Women's Watches**: Elegant women's watches for every occasion
- **Men's Watches**: Stylish men's watches and timepieces
- **Men's Necklaces**: Men's chain necklaces and pendants
- **Handbags & Purses**: Curated handbags, clutches and purses
- **Sunglasses**: Women's and men's sunglasses
- **Men's Sunglasses**: Stylish men's sunglasses
- **Scarves & Shawls**: Elegant scarves, shawls and ponchos
- **Perfume & Scents**: Curated fragrances and scents
- **Gift Packages**: Ready-made gift boxes for every occasion
- **Add-Ons**: Complementary items and accessories

## Product Features
- Hypoallergenic materials (nickel-free, lead-free)
- Waterproof and tarnish-resistant options
- 18k gold-plated and stainless steel
- Premium gift packaging available
